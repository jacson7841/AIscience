# LM358-class signal conditioner: successful model-generated design

This directory contains the first model-generated candidate that passed every
deterministic LM358-class signal-conditioning check. It was produced by
`qwen3-coder-plus` in independent run 3, after three repair attempts.

## Verified results

| Check | Measured | Acceptance |
|---|---:|---:|
| Output bias | 2.499875 V | 2.25--2.75 V |
| Gain at 100 Hz | 4.974985 V/V | 4.5--5.5 V/V |
| Cutoff frequency | 1000 Hz | 700--1300 Hz |
| 100 Hz to 10 kHz attenuation | 19.9917 dB | at least 15 dB |
| Transient output amplitude | 0.497497 V | 0.40--0.60 V |
| Transient output range | 2.00238--2.99737 V | within 0.2--4.8 V |

`design.py` is the generated PySpice source. `result.json` and `response.png`
are produced by the deterministic checker. `circuit.cir` is the exported SPICE
netlist, `netlist_structure.json` is its parsed connectivity, and
`schematic.svg`/`schematic.png` are generated by SchemDraw from that connectivity.

This is an LM358-class ideal-VCVS abstraction, not a vendor LM358 macromodel.
The result therefore verifies topology and target response, but not slew rate,
input common-mode limits, load-dependent output swing, noise, or temperature drift.
